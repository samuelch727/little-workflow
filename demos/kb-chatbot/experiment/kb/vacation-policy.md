# Vacation policy

Northwind Systems, revision 1 (fictional sample document).

## Accrual

- Full-time employees accrue **15 working days** of paid vacation per calendar year.
- Accrual is monthly, at 1.25 days per completed month of service.
- Employees in their first 90 days accrue but may not take vacation.

## Requesting time off

- Requests go through the `#time-off` channel and must be approved by your direct manager.
- Requests of **5 consecutive working days or more** need **three weeks' notice**.
- Requests of fewer than 5 days need one week's notice.

## Carryover

- Unused vacation days **do not carry over**. The balance resets to zero on 1 January.
- Unused days are not paid out on departure.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the employee's registered work location and do not
count against the vacation balance.
