# Benefits overview

Northwind Systems, people team (fictional sample document).

A summary only. The scheme documents in the HR portal are authoritative for anything that
touches money or eligibility.

- **Pension** — Northwind matches employee contributions up to 6% of base salary.
- **Health** — private medical cover for the employee; partner and dependants can be added
  at cost during the annual enrolment window.
- **Learning budget** — an annual allowance for books, courses, and conferences. Spend it
  through the normal expense route.
- **Cycle to work** — available at every location with an office.

Leave entitlements (vacation, sickness, family leave) are covered by the HR policy
documents, not by this summary.
