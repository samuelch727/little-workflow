# Expense guide

Northwind Systems, finance operations (fictional sample document).

This guide covers **equipment and software purchases**. Travel and subsistence limits are
set regionally and are maintained by each region's finance team.

## Equipment and software

- Purchases **up to €500** need no prior approval — buy it and file the receipt.
- Purchases **over €500** need your direct manager's written approval before you buy.
- Anything over €2,500 additionally needs a finance business partner's sign-off.
- Software subscriptions are procurement's call regardless of price; open a `#procurement`
  request rather than paying with a personal card.

## Filing a claim

1. Photograph the receipt. A card statement line is not a receipt.
2. File in Expensify within **30 days** of the purchase date.
3. Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically and need a manager appeal.

## Not reimbursable

Personal travel, alcohol outside a client dinner, fines, and home broadband.
