# Expense guide

Northwind Systems finance team (fictional sample document).

## What is reimbursed

- Travel booked through the company travel desk.
- Meals while travelling, up to the per-diem limits below.
- Home-office equipment up to **$800** per employee per two years.
- Conference tickets when the conference is agreed with your manager in advance.

## Per-diem limits

| Region | Daily meal limit |
|---|---|
| North America | $75 |
| Europe | €65 |
| Rest of world | $60 |

Alcohol is not reimbursable and must be split out of the receipt.

## Filing a claim

- File within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
- Every claim over **$25** needs an itemised receipt; a card statement is not a receipt.
- Claims are paid with the next payroll run after approval.
