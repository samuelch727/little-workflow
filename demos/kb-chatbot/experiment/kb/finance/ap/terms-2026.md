# Accounts payable — supplier terms 2026

Northwind Systems, finance operations. (Fictional sample document.)

## Payment terms

- Standard supplier and vendor invoices are paid **net 30** from the invoice date.
- Net 45 applies to the three framework suppliers named in the procurement register.
- Early-payment discounts are taken where the discount beats 0.5% per month.
- Nothing is paid without a purchase-order number on the invoice. An invoice without a PO
  is returned to the supplier, not queued.

## Payment runs

Two runs a month, on the 10th and the 25th. An invoice approved after a run's cut-off waits
for the next one; there is no off-cycle payment except for a supplier stop-ship.

## Disputes

Raise with the finance BP within 5 working days of receipt. A disputed invoice is held, and
the hold is recorded against the PO so the supplier's statement reconciles.
