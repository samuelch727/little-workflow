# Glossary

Northwind Systems internal terms (fictional sample document).

- **Aurora** — the customer-facing web application.
- **Beacon** — the internal metrics pipeline.
- **BP** — business partner, as in "finance BP".
- **Cutover** — the window in which a customer moves from a trial to a paid plan.
- **SEV** — severity, as used in incident management.
- **T&S** — travel and subsistence.
- **WP** — the workplace team, who run the offices.
