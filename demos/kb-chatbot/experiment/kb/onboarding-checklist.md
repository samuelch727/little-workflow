# Onboarding checklist

Your first week at Northwind Systems (fictional sample document).

## Day 1

- Collect your laptop and hardware key from IT.
- Sign in to the password manager and store your credentials there.
- Join `#general`, `#announcements`, and your team channel.

## Day 2–3

- Meet your onboarding buddy.
- Read your team's handbook section.
- Complete the security training module.

## End of week 1

- 1:1 with your manager to agree your first-month goals.
- Confirm your payroll and benefits enrolment in the HR portal.
