# Office access

Northwind Systems, workplace team (fictional sample document).

## Badge hours

- Employee badges are active **07:00–20:00** on weekdays.
- Outside those hours, and at weekends, badge access needs a workplace-team exception
  raised in `#workplace`.

## Guests and visitors

- Register a guest in the visitor system at least **24 hours** before they arrive.
- A registered guest still needs an employee host to meet them at reception.
- Unregistered visitors cannot be badged in on the day. There is no exception for
  candidates or contractors.

## Wifi

- `northwind-corp` — for managed laptops. Certificate-based, no password.
- `northwind-guest` — for visitors. The rotating password is printed on the card at
  reception.
