# Time off (2023) — ARCHIVED

Northwind Systems, people team. Revision 2, effective 1 January 2023. **Superseded** by the
2026 revision. Kept for reference only; do not quote from this document.

- Full-time employees accrue 18 working days of paid vacation per calendar year.
- Up to 3 unused days carry over. Carried-over days expire on 31 January.
- Requests of 5 consecutive working days or more need two weeks' notice.
