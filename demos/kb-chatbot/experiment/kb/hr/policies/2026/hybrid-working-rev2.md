# Hybrid working

Northwind Systems, people team. Revision 2, effective **1 January 2026**. Supersedes the
root-level remote-work page and the 2023 archive copy. (Fictional sample document.)

## Days in the office

- Employees within commuting distance of an office come in **3 days a week**.
- **Core days are Tuesday and Thursday.** Everyone in a team is in on those two days; the
  third day is the team's choice and is recorded in the team charter.
- Fully remote contracts need the VP of the function **and** the people team, and are
  reviewed annually rather than granted indefinitely.

## Home office allowance

- **€400 every two years** for home working equipment — desk, chair, monitor stand, lamp.
  The allowance is per employee, not per item, and does not roll over.
- Laptops, hardware keys, and monitors issued by IT are outside the allowance.
- Claim it in Expensify against the `home-office` category, with a receipt.

## Office travel

Travel to your registered office is not expensable. Travel to another office is a business
trip and follows that region's travel and subsistence limits.
