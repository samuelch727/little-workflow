# Time off

Northwind Systems, people team. Revision 3, effective **1 January 2026**. Supersedes every
earlier time-off document. (Fictional sample document.)

## Vacation accrual

- Full-time employees accrue **20 working days** of paid vacation per calendar year.
- Accrual is monthly, at **1.67 days** per completed month of service.
- Part-time employees accrue pro rata on contracted hours.
- Employees in their first 90 days accrue but may not take vacation.

## Carryover

- Up to **5 unused vacation days** carry over into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days **expire on 31 March**. After that date they are removed from the
  balance without notice.
- Unused days beyond the 5-day cap are forfeited and are not paid out on departure.

## Requesting time off

- Requests go through the `#time-off` channel and need your direct manager's approval.
- Requests of 5 consecutive working days or more need three weeks' notice.
- Requests of fewer than 5 days need one week's notice.

## Sickness

Sickness is not vacation and does not draw down the vacation balance. Report sickness to
your manager on the first morning; a doctor's note is needed from the fourth consecutive
working day.
