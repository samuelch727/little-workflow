# Remote work

Northwind Systems, people team. Revision 1, effective 1 March 2021 (fictional sample
document).

## Where you work

- Employees within commuting distance of an office come in **two days per week**. Which two
  days is between you and your team; there are no fixed days.
- Fully remote contracts are by exception, agreed with the VP of the function.
- Teams may agree their own meeting days. The people team does not set them.

## Home working equipment

- A home desk setup — desk, chair, monitor stand — is claimable **once every three years**,
  up to **€250**.
- Laptops and hardware keys come from IT and are not part of this allowance.
- Claims go through the normal expense route.

## Travel to the office

Travel to your registered office is not expensable. Travel to another office is a business
trip and follows the regional travel limits.
