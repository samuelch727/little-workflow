The Little stack is for agents that get better from the conversations they
have. **LittleDB** is the product: it traces what your agents do, scores it, and
improves them. **Little Harness** and **Little Workflow** are the SDK you build
those agents and workflows with. **Workspaces** are where an agent's work lives:
each user's own folder, ready when they return, managed by LittleDB's hosted
service or self-hosted with Little Compute.

Start with Little Harness. Add Little Workflow only when a job must be bounded,
durable and replayable. Connect LittleDB when you have access to it.
