# Lost or stolen laptop

Northwind Systems, IT security. Incident procedure. (Fictional sample document.)

Applies to a laptop that is lost, stolen, left behind, or otherwise out of your control —
including a device left in a taxi, a train, or a hotel room.

## Reporting

- Report to `security@northwind.example` within **4 hours** of noticing the device is gone.
  There is no exception for weekends or holidays.
- File incident form **SEC-19** in the same message. A report without SEC-19 does not start
  the remote-wipe clock.
- If the device held customer data, say so in the first line. That changes the handling
  path and the regulator clock.

## What happens next

1. Security remote-wipes the device and revokes its certificates and sessions.
2. Your manager is notified. You are not charged for the hardware.
3. A replacement is shipped within two business days.
4. Change your password if the device was unlocked when it was lost.

## What not to do

Do not use "find my device" tracking to attempt recovery in person. Do not wait until the
next working day to report.
