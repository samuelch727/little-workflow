# Appendix C — family leave

Northwind Systems, people team. Appendix to the 2026 time-off policy. (Fictional sample
document.)

## Parental leave

- A new parent — birth, adoption, or surrogacy, and regardless of gender — is entitled to
  **16 weeks** of paid parental leave in the 12 months following the birth or placement.
- Leave may be taken in up to three blocks. Blocks shorter than two weeks need the
  manager's agreement.
- Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary and does not draw down the vacation
  balance.
- Notice: tell your manager and the people team at least **8 weeks** before the intended
  start date where that is possible.

## Carer's leave

Five paid days per calendar year for the care of a dependant. No notice period; tell your
manager as soon as you can.

## Bereavement

Ten paid days on the death of a partner, child, or parent. Five paid days otherwise. The
people team will not ask for documentation.
