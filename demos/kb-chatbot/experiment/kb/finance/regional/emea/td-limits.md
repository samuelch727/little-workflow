# EMEA travel and subsistence limits

Northwind Systems, EMEA finance. Effective 1 January 2026. (Fictional sample document.)

Applies to travel within the EMEA region, including Berlin, Amsterdam, Dublin, and London.

## Daily limits

- **Meals: €65 per day.** This is a daily total, not a per-meal limit, and it does not need
  itemising below the daily cap.
- **Hotel: €180 per night**, city-centre rates included. Above the cap needs the trip
  owner's approval before booking, not after.
- Local transport: actuals, receipted. Taxis where public transport is impractical or
  unsafe.

## Flights

- Economy for flights under six hours. Premium economy above six hours.
- Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip is known that far in advance.

## Claiming

- File in Expensify within 30 days, with the trip name in the report title.
- A day on which the company paid for a meal (a client dinner, a conference lunch) reduces
  that day's meal allowance proportionately.

Other regions publish their own limits; do not apply these figures outside EMEA.
